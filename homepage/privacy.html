Privacy.

LAST UPDATED: 2026-09-17

What we store

  • Account — username, an argon2id-hashed password, optional email, login timestamps, and audit log entries.
  • Flows & deployments — the canvas graph you author, the parameters you choose, the public contract address you deploy to Stellar, and the on-chain event stream we index.
  • Passkeys — public WebAuthn credentials. We never see your private key.

What we do not store

  • blockYour Stellar secret key. Paiflow is non-custodial.
  • blockCard details. We don’t take payments.

Third parties

Stellar Horizon and Soroban RPC see the public network traffic required to read or submit your transactions. Optionally, error traces are sent to Sentry; this is off unless the operator configures SENTRY_DSN.

Product analytics are sent to PostHog (US) while the app is in beta. We use them to see where people get stuck. While you are signed in they are tied to an internal account ID, never your username or email. Secret keys, transaction payloads and the addresses you pay to are stripped from analytics events before they leave your browser. The public address of the wallet you sign with is included, with the transaction hash, so a transaction can be traced back to the account that signed it. A transaction you sign without being signed in is recorded under that public wallet address alone, with no account or profile behind it. We do not record your screen or your session.
