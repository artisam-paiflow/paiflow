Privacy.

LAST UPDATED: 2026-09-15

What we store

  • Account — username, an argon2id-hashed password, optional email, login timestamps, and audit log entries.
  • Flows & deployments — the canvas graph you author, the parameters you choose, the public contract address you deploy to Stellar, and the on-chain event stream we index.
  • Passkeys — public WebAuthn credentials. We never see your private key.

What we do not store

  • blockYour Stellar secret key. Paiflow is non-custodial.
  • blockCard details. We don’t take payments.

Third parties

Stellar Horizon and Soroban RPC see the public network traffic required to read or submit your transactions. Optionally, error traces are sent to Sentry; this is off unless the operator configures SENTRY_DSN.

On our beta test environment only, product analytics and session recordings are sent to PostHog (US). We use them to see where testers get stuck. They are tied to an internal account ID, never your username or email. Recordings mask all on-screen text and form inputs, and Stellar addresses and transaction payloads are stripped from analytics events before they leave your browser. The public app does not load analytics.
