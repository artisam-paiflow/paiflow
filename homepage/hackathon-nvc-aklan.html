NVC Aklan Hackathon

Inside the lab at NVC Aklan, where students came together for a day of building with Paiflow.

October 20, 2026Northwestern Visayan Colleges · AklanSTELLAR TESTNET

/ THE CHALLENGE

Build for someone. Show the flow.

The Aklan challenge invited teams to build an app around a useful payment experience: explain who pays, why they pay, and who receives the money. Paiflow provided the payment-flow builder and integration resources.

The program combined an introduction to Paiflow, time to finish integrations, and project pitches. Teams were asked to demonstrate their app and a confirmed payout on Stellar testnet.
